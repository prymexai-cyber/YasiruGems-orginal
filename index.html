THE JEWELLER

Two decades of Sri Lankan craftsmanship.

Yasiru Gems & Jewellery is a Colombo-based fine jewellery house with over 20 years of experience sourcing, cutting and setting authentic Ceylon gemstones — sapphires, rubies, spinels and more — by hand.

Every piece is backed by an official Silver License from the Sri Lankan National Gem and Jewellery Authority (NGJA), so clients can buy with full confidence in the origin and authenticity of every stone.
