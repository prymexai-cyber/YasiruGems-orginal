WHAT WE DO

Handmade, stone by stone.

OUR CRAFT

From rough stone to finished piece.

Every service is grounded in NGJA-licensed sourcing and two decades of hand craftsmanship.

Custom Jewellery

Bespoke rings, necklaces and earrings designed around a chosen gemstone or a client's own vision — from sketch to finished piece.

  • Engagement & wedding rings
  • Bespoke necklaces & earrings
  • Design consultation included

Gem Sourcing & Certification

Loose gemstones sourced directly from licensed Sri Lankan markets, graded and sold with full transparency on origin and quality.

  • Sapphires, rubies & rare stones
  • NGJA-compliant trading
  • Certification guidance on request

Repairs & Restoration

Resizing, re-setting and restoration for existing jewellery — old family pieces brought back to their original brilliance.

  • Ring resizing & repair
  • Stone re-setting
  • Heirloom restoration
