OUR STORY

Yasiru Gems & Jewellery

Yasiru Gems & Jewellery began in Colombo over two decades ago, out of a personal fascination with the sapphires, rubies and rare stones that make Sri Lanka one of the world's great gem-producing islands. What started as an apprenticeship in gem cutting grew into a full jewellery house, built one hand-set piece at a time.

Yasiru holds an official Silver License from the Sri Lankan National Gem and Jewellery Authority (NGJA) — the government body responsible for regulating and certifying the country's gem and jewellery trade. This license reflects over 20 years of verified experience and a commitment to ethical sourcing and honest grading of every stone sold.

Today, every ring, necklace and loose gem leaving the workshop carries that same standard: authentic Ceylon origin, transparent pricing, and craftsmanship done entirely by hand.
